ABOUT THE GIANTS

Angono Giants was developed as an entry for TechFiesta at National University. Created by INF232, inspired by the Higantes Festival.

The Higantes Festival is a local festival held annually in Angono, Rizal, Philippines, where hundreds of giant papier-mâché puppets are paraded, representing the common people's mockery of the bad hacienda owners of the past during Spanish colonial rule.
